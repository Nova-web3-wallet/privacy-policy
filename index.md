1. Introduction

Nova Web3 Extension ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our browser extension wallet handles your data.
2. Data We Do NOT Collect

- Your private keys or seed phrases
- Your wallet addresses or transaction history
- Your IP address or browsing activity
- Personal identification information (unless you provide it)
- Cookies or tracking identifiers

3. Data Stored Locally

All sensitive data is stored exclusively on your device:
- Private keys and seed phrases (encrypted)
- Account names and settings
- Transaction history
- Address book entries

This data never leaves your device without your explicit action.

4. Data We May Collect

Crash Reports & Analytics (Optional):
- App crashes and errors (if enabled)
- Feature usage (anonymized)
- Performance metrics

Blockchain Interactions:
- RPC requests to blockchain nodes (your IP may be visible to RPC providers)
- We do not log or store these requests

5. Third-Party Services

We may use:
- RPC Providers (Polygon, Ethereum, Bitcoin, etc.) - they may see your IP
- Ledger/Hardware Wallets - for secure key management
- Web3 dApps - you control what data you share

6. Security

- Your private keys are encrypted and never transmitted
- You have full custody of your funds
- We recommend using hardware wallets for large holdings

7. User Control

You can:
- Export or delete your wallet anytime
- Disable analytics
- Review all permissions granted to dApps
- Disconnect from any service at any time

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Continued use means you accept changes.
